How many minutes did you spend looking at other people's photographs yesterday?

We read other people's stories by the dozen, every day. The time to look back on our own keeps getting put off.
Take a little of the time you spend on other people's memories, and give it back to your own.

Narration
Two teacups on the veranda

The late sun traces the weave of the tatami and draws the shadow of the screens out long. Past the opened doors, laundry hangs on the line, and the garden is green. The sun has come down to the height of the roof, and the wooden floor has turned to gold.

One teacup near, one further off. As though someone had been sitting there a moment ago.

This story came from the photograph.
In the app, the words you speak are what it is made from.
