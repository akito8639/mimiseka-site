Looking back, and giving

What you really wanted to keep from that day.
——Say it, and it comes back.

Memory fades as the days go by.
Words you put into a voice do not.

Looking back

The days you have half-forgotten are the ones it helps you reach.

The home screen holds Today and Looking back. Looking back quietly offers you the same season a year ago, or a photo you have not opened in a while.

To search, use the run of dates, the calendar, the map or the region. You can arrive from “that autumn” or from “that town”. Under each photo sit the date and time it was taken, and the name of the place.

Every way of viewing the bookshelf — home, cards, list, calendar, map, region — is available on every plan.

Giving

The person receiving it needs no app.

Turn it into a photograph you listen to and you get a QR code and a link. All they do is point an ordinary phone camera at it, and the reading begins in a browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

Send it from LINE, post it on X, hand it to another app. Each of these works as it is. You choose how long each photo stays published, and you can stop it at any time.

It can go on a printed photograph too. A picture on the wall, or one in an album, becomes a memory you can listen to.

The same photograph, for people who cannot see

The description is written into the photograph itself. So whether it is posted on social media or sitting in the Photos app, that one picture comes across in a reading voice.

With the ears and the fingertips, all the way through — on accessibility →

Your own story begins.
