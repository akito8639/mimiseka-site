Letters arrive.

One a day, from the you of another day. One a month, from Scia.
And the description written into each photo gets a choice of tone.

A letter for today

From the you of another day, one a day

Press Journaling on the home screen and you find a desk where letters arrive. Every evening at ten, one envelope. The sender is the you of another day.

Your diary from this day a year ago. A day where a conversation stopped halfway. A day at the same place, or under the same weather. The words you left that day come back on a sheet of paper, exactly as you said them. Nothing is summarised, nothing is rephrased.

Delivered 7 October To you,
Home for the first time in a while. Tea with my mother on the veranda. We weren't talking about anything in particular, and still, we both laughed about when to bring the laundry in. It was only that kind of evening, but I think it was enough. From you, a year ago

Open breaks the seal. Read aloud reads it in your reading voice. A letter from a day where the talking stopped carries Pick up the conversation, and takes you straight back into it.

A letter you did not open lasts only that day. At ten the next evening, the next one takes its place. Rather than piling them up and counting them, we put the value in the one.

Until you have three chapters or so, Scia's first letter sits on the desk. Take one photo, and it becomes the seed of the next. No plan is required.

A letter for the month

From Scia, one a month

For the first seven days of a new month, Last month in review appears on the home screen. At the top of it sits a letter from Scia.

Drawn from last month's diaries and from what you said about your photos, it is a friend who was looking at the pictures with you, thinking back over the month and speaking to you: two or three hundred characters. Photos you only took, with no words, are added as background: the date, the place, and what is in the frame.

Press Listen to the letter and it is read in Scia's voice. Scia's voice is chosen separately from the story and guidance voices, under “Pick the reading voice” in Settings.

On the same page are the photos you kept coming back to last month, and one photo from the same month a year ago. Bind these into a book turns the month into a book as it is.

With a paid plan it arrives every month. On the free plan it arrives for the first three months. The notification is Last month's letter in Settings: one a month, at the start of the month.

Two tones for the description

The description written into each photo has only ever used polite, formal Japanese. From 1.3.0 you can choose Friendly: the spoken Japanese of a teacher talking to young children.

Polite白いマグカップが、窓からの光を受けています。奥には観葉植物があります。
Friendly白いマグカップがあるよ。窓から光が入ってるよ。奥には、みどりの葉っぱがあるね。

Switch it from the Description pill on the camera screen. Below the length (brief, standard, detailed) sit the two tones (polite, friendly). The Description row of the style sheet offers the same choice. If you change nothing, it stays polite, as before.

A friendly description can have a voice of its own. When the description voice is an AI voice, a row called Friendly description voice appears under “Description voice” in Settings. Pick a voice that suits the tone, and the reading right after the shot, and the chapter's reading later, both use it.

The tone applies only to the description written into the photo. The wording of diaries and stories does not change. Descriptions are written in Japanese, so the tone applies to that Japanese text.
